Standard English Conventions

Digital SAT Reading and Writing Practice and Mastery

When two answers both sound fine, knowing the rule makes the difference. This workbook helps you see the structure beneath a sentence and understand why one answer works.

Build your skills with focused explanations and guided walkthroughs. Practise individual rules, apply them in longer passages, then bring everything together in cumulative review and the 25-question Grammar Gauntlet.

A way forward, one rule at a time

Understand it. Try it. Make it stick.

01 / Get the idea

Clarity before complexity.

Start with explanations written for high school students. Learn what to look for, with examples that show the rule in action.

02 / Work through it

Practise with purpose.

Follow a guided walkthrough, then work independently. Move from focused skill practice to questions that combine what you’ve learned.

03 / Learn from it

Make every answer useful.

Worked solutions explain the correct answer and the other choices. Use them to find your next step, whether you got the question right or wrong.

Inside Volume 1

The rules behind better decisions.

From sentence boundaries to verb forms, each chapter gives you another tool for approaching an unfamiliar sentence.

  1. Clause boundaries and punctuation
  2. Relative clauses, appositives and nonessential modifiers
  3. Conjunctions, transitions and sentence joining
  4. Misplaced and dangling modifiers
  5. Subject–verb agreement
  6. Pronoun agreement, clarity and case
  7. Verb tense, aspect and mood
  8. Possessives, plurals and apostrophes
  9. Cumulative review
  10. The Grammar Gauntlet
